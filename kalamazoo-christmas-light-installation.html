Kalamazoo Christmas Light Installation FAQ

How much does professional installation cost?

Residential packages generally begin around $800. Your final quote depends on the property, roofline, features, access, and design complexity. Commercial projects are custom quoted.

What happens if a light goes out?

Contact us. Our season-long service guarantee covers H&R-supplied lights and our installation if they need attention during the display season.

Do you decorate commercial properties?

Yes. We have experience with large and detailed installations, including coordinated multi-building and tall-structure displays.

Are removal and storage included?

Removal is part of our complete seasonal service. Optional storage is available to simplify preparation for the following year.
