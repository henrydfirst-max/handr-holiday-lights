Portage Christmas Light Installation FAQ

What is included with installation?

Custom design, commercial-grade LED lights, insured installation, in-season service, removal, and optional storage are all available through H&R.

As early as possible. Booking early gives Portage customers the best selection of dates before the seasonal schedule becomes limited.

Yes. We design warm-white, multicolor, and custom color-pattern displays based on your home and preferences.
