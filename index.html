About H&R Holiday Lights

H&R Holiday Lights LLC is locally owned and operated by Henry and Rachel. We create custom residential and commercial Christmas light displays throughout Southwest Michigan.

With more than 300 completed residential and commercial installations, we provide commercial-grade lights, insured installation, seasonal maintenance, removal, and optional storage for a simple, dependable experience.
