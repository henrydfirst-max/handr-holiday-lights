Residential project spotlight

Creating Christmas Magic for a New Home

The homeowners came to H&R looking for something that would brighten their new home and make the Christmas season feel especially memorable for their family. After seeing the custom designs available, they decided to create a complete display rather than stop with a simple roofline.

Goal
Bring warmth and Christmas magic to a new home
Display
Detailed roofline, illuminated trees, and garland
Relationship
Trusted H&R customer for three holiday seasons

H&R used a consistent warm-white palette across the home’s upper and lower rooflines, peaks, trees, and entry details. When the display came on, the homeowners immediately loved how it highlighted the architecture and welcomed their family. Three seasons later, H&R continues to recreate and maintain the display they fell in love with.
