Frequently Asked Questions

Are you insured?

Yes. H&R Holiday Lights is fully insured, so you can have peace of mind knowing your property is protected while we install and service your holiday lighting.

Do you provide the lights?

Yes! We supply high-quality commercial-grade LED lights designed to look beautiful and last throughout the holiday season.

Is it cheaper if we provide the lights?

All lighting used for our installations is supplied by H&R Holiday Lights, ensuring that we can provide a consistent, professional, and reliable installation

Do I need to have an outdoor outlet available for my holiday lights?

Yes. Customers are responsible for providing a working outdoor electrical outlet for their holiday lighting installation. The outlet must be accessible and able to safely power the lighting display. If you have any concerns about your outlet, please let us know when requesting your quote.

What colors can I choose from?

We have 10 different colors you can mix and match we suggest referencing our work page for ideas.

Do you install lights on two-story homes?

Absolutely. Our team has the equipment and experience to safely install lights on homes of many sizes.

Do you remove the lights after the holidays?

Yes. We return after the season to carefully remove your lights and leave your home looking just as we found it.

Do you store the lights?

Yes. We can safely store your holiday lights so they are ready for next season.

How much does holiday lighting cost?

Every home is different. We provide free, no-obligation estimates based on your home's size and the lighting design you choose.

When should I schedule my installation?

We recommend scheduling as early as possible to reserve your preferred installation date before the holiday rush.
