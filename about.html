About H&R Holiday Lights

Meet Henry and Rachel, the local owners behind more than 300 completed installations.

How It Started

H&R Holiday Lights began with our very first holiday lighting installation. What started as a small business has grown into a company serving homeowners and businesses throughout Southwest Michigan.

Since then, we have completed more than 300 residential and commercial installations. Each season, we continue to build on that experience while helping more customers create beautiful holiday displays.

Who We Are

H&R Holiday Lights LLC is owned and operated by Henry and Rachel. As co-owners, we work together to provide dependable design, installation, seasonal service, removal, and storage for homes and businesses throughout Southwest Michigan.

Through H&R Holiday Lights, we have completed more than 300 residential and commercial installations. That hands-on experience guides how we design each display, care for each property, and communicate with every customer.

Outside the business, Henry is pursuing his Ph.D. in Plant Pathology and Rachel is studying to become a Dental Hygienist. Building H&R together gives us the opportunity to make the holiday season easier and more memorable for our customers.

What We Believe

We believe holiday lighting should be beautiful, professional, and hassle-free.

Every property is different, so we work with each customer to create a display that fits their home or business. From the initial design to installation and removal, our goal is to make the entire process simple for our customers.

Our Commitment

We take pride in providing high-quality work, dependable service, and exceptional customer care.

Whether we're decorating a family home, business, church, or larger commercial property, we approach every project with attention to detail and a commitment to doing the job right.
